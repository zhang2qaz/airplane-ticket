01 — Who We Are

A product company at heart.
An engineering partner by craft.

Most software companies are founded first and go looking for a product afterwards. Ascham did it the other way around. Our founding team — senior engineers and product leaders from global technology companies — spent two full years in R&D building Competitor IQ before Ascham Inc. was formally incorporated in the United States in 2026, headquartered in New York.

That sequence shaped everything about us. Our engineering standards were forged in production, with live enterprise pilots — not in slide decks. When we incorporated, we already had a hardened platform, a proven delivery practice, and pilot customers who had renewed twice.

03 — Competitor IQ · How It Works

From market noise to your next move.

One pipeline, three stages — engineered so that nothing important in your market happens without your team knowing, understanding, and acting on it.
01 — COLLECT

Watch everything

30+ source types, monitored continuously: competitor websites and pricing pages, app stores, review platforms, job boards, patents, press, social and regulatory filings.

02 — UNDERSTAND

Make it mean something

The Signal Engine dedupes, classifies and ranks over a million raw events a month, then writes source-cited summaries — so analysts verify in one click instead of hunting for hours.

03 — ACT

Move first

Always-fresh battlecards for sales, instant alerts in Slack and Teams, a weekly executive digest, and an open API that feeds your own workflows.

07 — How We Work

Trust is an engineering deliverable.

Delivery you can see

  • Small senior pods — 3–5 engineers, led hands-on, no hand-offs between “sales team” and “delivery team”.
  • A demo every week — working software from week one; roadmap, burn-down and risks visible in a shared dashboard.
  • Your codebase, your cloud — everything in your repos and your accounts from the first commit.

Security by default

  • Aligned with ISO 27001 & SOC 2 practices — access reviews, least privilege, secrets management, audit trails.
  • Encryption everywhere — in transit and at rest, for our product and for every client system we touch.
  • Clean IP, clean exits — clear IP assignment, documented handover, no lock-in by design.

Quality you can audit

  • 100% peer-reviewed code — nothing merges without a second senior engineer’s approval.
  • Tested and observable — automated test suites, CI/CD pipelines and production observability on every build.
  • Documentation as a habit — architecture decisions recorded as we go, not reconstructed at the end.
