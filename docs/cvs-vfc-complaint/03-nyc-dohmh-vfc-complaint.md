**Facts.** On [date], I asked staff at the CVS Pharmacy at 2833 Broadway, New York, NY 10025 in person whether the store participates in the VFC program; a staff member confirmed it does. I then booked a vaccination appointment for my child ([vaccine], July 30, 2026) through CVS's online system (confirmation attached). At the appointment, the pharmacy staff member on duty denied ever having heard of VFC, demanded that I provide insurance or pay for the vaccination, cut off my attempt to explain the program, told me "there is no VFC here — contact VFC yourself and figure it out," and refused to vaccinate my child. **Requests.**

2. **If it is enrolled**, I ask the PQA Unit to investigate this as a provider-agreement compliance matter. As I understand the program requirements, an enrolled provider must screen for VFC eligibility at each immunization visit, may not charge for VFC vaccine, must waive the administration fee (capped at $25.10 per dose) where a family cannot afford it, and may not refuse to vaccinate an eligible child because of inability to pay. Each of those requirements appears to have been violated here — including a categorical denial that the program exists.
3. **If it is not enrolled**, please note for your records that this store's staff affirmatively confirmed VFC participation to a parent and its scheduling system accepted a pediatric appointment on that basis — misinformation that cost my child a timely vaccination. I have raised this with CVS corporate and with the NY Attorney General's Health Care Bureau.
4. Either way, I would appreciate a referral to the nearest location where my child can promptly receive [vaccine] under VFC.
